"Cousin C. brought the tiny dog to see me — a ravishing animal." That comes first, before anything else in the day gets its say, and it is the only thing in the entry you did not qualify.

Then: "The same despairing desire to work, and could not work. I suppose I started reviewing T. nine or ten times." The "same" is doing a lot there — it means this was not news to you, that you recognised it on arrival. And "I suppose," as if the count of nine or ten had not been kept so much as noticed afterwards, from a distance. Nine or ten beginnings is a strange thing to have to say about a day; you say it in half a line and move on to being tired.

What I keep returning to is the order. The dog is at the top. Not as consolation, not set against the rest — just there first, ravishing, with no argument attached to it.
