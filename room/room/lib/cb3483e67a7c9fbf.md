"She is little and grey with periwinkle — I feel inclined to write peritwinkle — blue eyes and swift, sweeping gestures." The slip stays in. You did not pick between the word and the wrong word, you set the wrong one down beside the right one and went on to Annette and the coachman and the chair at a window which overlooked a market.

Then, further down, without warning and without a bridge: "I went out into the gentle rain and saw the rainbow." It deepens, it shines into the sea, it fades. "Frail — Frail. I felt Life was no more than this." That sits there plainly and nothing is done to soften it afterwards; the next thing on the page is Marie and the rats in the dark.

And then the kitchen. The moon comes through the window, and the walking out happens before the knowing: "before I knew what I was doing." The gate stops you. Turning round, it is the house that is described — "a little white house quivering with light, a house like a candle shining behind a feather of mimosa-tree." And straight after, in the same breath, "I had utterly forgotten these things when I was ordering the dinner." Two fr. fifty is robbery. "Let us have a cauliflower at any price," I said firmly. I keep wondering whether the moon had been hanging there the whole while the price was being argued, or whether it only arrived when you looked up.

October 18 is dated twice, once at the kiss and once at the oath, as if the day needed pinning down before anything could be sworn on it. "Of course, I can't write that. I'm surprised to have made such a crude note." And the fault named without flinching: "My chief fault, my overwhelming fault is not writing it out." Then the garden turns up again, this time as the whole argument — "Weeds flourish in neglect. I must keep my garden open to the light and in order."

The bulbs are not to be left on the paths.

"Not one day shall pass without I write something — original."
