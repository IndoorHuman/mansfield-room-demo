# Illness MOC

She was diagnosed with tuberculosis in 1917 and died of it in January 1923, aged
34. Everything after 1917 — every address, nearly every decision — is organised
around it. So is nearly all of the work she is remembered for.

## What it actually looked like
Bandol, San Remo, Menton, Montana-sur-Sierre, Sierre, Fontainebleau: a list of
places she went to be less ill. Between them: X-ray treatments, a great deal of
lying still, and *Bliss* and *The Garden Party*.

## Where it is in this vault
- The **[[The Journal MOC|diary]]** carries it plainly — it is fenced, so the
  room never surfaces it directly.
- The **[[Letters MOC|letters]]** carry it obliquely; she was careful with
  people, and reported weather instead of symptoms.
- **[[The Man without a Temperament]]** and **[[The Fly]]** carry it as art.

## The line worth keeping
> "I adore Life, but my experience of the world is that it's pretty terrible."
> *(verbatim)*
