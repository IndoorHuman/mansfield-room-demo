My room is horrible. Very noisy: a constant clatter and a feeling as though it were doorless. French people don't care a hang how much noise they make. I hate them for it. Stayed in bed; felt very ill, but don't mind because of the reason. The food was

really appalling: nothing to eat. At night old Casetta feelings, like madness. Voices and words and halfvisions.
