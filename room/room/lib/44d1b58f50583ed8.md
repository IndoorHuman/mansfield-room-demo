# On Shakespeare

> Authored reading note. Quotations **verbatim** from the 1929 *Letters* in
> this vault. Nothing here is drawn from the fenced diary.

Shakespeare is the steadiest reading of her last years — daily, ordinary,
kept up through everything.

> "I have been reading Shakespeare as usual."

She read him with a pen, chasing every unfamiliar word:

> "I have been steeped in Shakespeare these last days with a note book —
> looking up every word, finding what are inkles and caddises...."

> "I have started a new Shakespeare note book."

She read him aloud, with other people, as part of a working day:

> "The hours away from it we read Shakespeare aloud, discuss what has been
> written."

And she kept him within reach of the bed she spent so much of her life in — in
what may be the best single sentence in the letters:

> "On my bed at night there is a copy of Shakespeare, a copy of Chaucer, an
> automatic pistol and a black muslin fan."

She also argued with him:

> "That Leontes is an intolerable man and I hate gentle Hermione."
