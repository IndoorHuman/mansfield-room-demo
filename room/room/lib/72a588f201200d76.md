All this! All that I write — all that I am — is on the border of the sea. It's a kind of playing. I want to put all my force behind it, but somehow, I cannot!

Journal IQ2I

Ful gay was al the ground, and queynt, And poudred as men had it peynt With many a fresh and sondry flour That casten up ful good savour.

[From an unposted letter.] It's a chill, strange day. I can just get about. I decided this morning to write to S about the Swiss

Spahlinger treatment: whether it would be suitable for me, etc. And I shall wire you to-morrow, asking

you to go and see S. Say what you like. But let

him know that I am practically a hopeless invalid. I have tried to explain about money to him; why I haven't paid him, and I have promised to pay the first moment I can....
