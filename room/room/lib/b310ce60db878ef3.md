Nuit blanche. Decided at 3 a.m. that D. was a homicidal maniac. Certain of this. Started my story, Late Spring.' A cold bitter day. Worked on Tchehov all day and then at my story till 11 p.m. Anna came. We talked about her to her face in English. No letters. Post Office strike. Anna's bow and velvet blouse.
