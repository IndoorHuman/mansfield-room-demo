"Foster came: says my lung is remarkably better, but must rest absolutely for two months and not attempt to walk at all." The good news and the sentence that takes it away arrive in the same breath, joined by a comma, and you did not stop to separate them. Then the quotation marks you put around your own borrowed phrase — I have got a " bigger chance." — the marks doing the work of holding it slightly away from you, as though it were said by someone else and you were only reporting it.

After that the entry stops explaining. "Bell rang at night. My eye pains me." "Cannot get a move on."

And then, with nothing to introduce it: "Dreamed about B. She gave me her baby to mind." It is the only sentence in the page with another person acting in it, and the only one where something is handed over rather than taken away. I keep wondering whether it came to you in that order — the lung, the bell, the eye, and last the dream — or whether the dream had been sitting there all day waiting for the rest to be written down first.
