Pinker writes to say The Nation has taken " The Doll's House."

1 See entry of October 18, 1920.

Journal IQ22

I am sure that meditation is the cure for the sickness of my mind, i.e. its lack of control. I have a terribly sensitive mind which receives every impression, and that is the reason why I am so carried away and borne under.
