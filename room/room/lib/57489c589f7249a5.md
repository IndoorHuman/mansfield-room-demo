# Things she loved

> Every line below is **verbatim** from the 1929 *Letters* in this vault.
> Nothing here is drawn from the fenced diary. The groupings are the only
> authored part.

## Looking at people
> "Oh, how I love the appearance of people — how I delight in it, if I love them."

## A place, known all the way through
> "And I love to be in a place all the year round, to know it in all its changes."

> "I love this place more and more."

## Small exact things
> "I love the satiny colour of mushrooms, and their smell and the soft stalks."

## Christmas
> "I love Christmas; I shall always feel it is a holy time."

## Gentleness
> "I love gentleness."

## Being a worker
> "How I love to think of us as workers, writers — two creatures given over to Art."

## What she actually wanted
> "I want flowers, rather sandy soil, green fields, and a river not too deep to
> paddle in, also a large number of ancient books and a small but very pretty cow."

## Beauty that is attached to something
> "I want in fact the feeling that all this beauty is in the deepest sense
> attached to life."

## Goodness, and life itself
> "And I long for goodness — to live by what is permanent in the soul."

> "I adore Life, but my experience of the world is that it's pretty terrible."
