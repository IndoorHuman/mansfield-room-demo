"Black. A day spent in Hell. Unable to do anything. Took brandy. Determined not to weep — wept." The sentences get shorter as they go, and then the sentence breaks in the middle: "I shall die if I don't" — and then, on its own line, "The Sea" — and then "escape."

The page is not tidy about it. Something came in and stood between the wanting and the word for it, and instead of going back and mending the line, you kept both halves and the thing that interrupted them.

After "Nauseated, faint, cold with misery" there is one more clause, and it is the only one turned outward: "Oh, I must survive it somehow."

I am not going to say anything over the top of that. It is written down plainly and it can stay plain here.
