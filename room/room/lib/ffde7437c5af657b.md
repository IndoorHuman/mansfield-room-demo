"Pinker writes to say The Nation has taken " The Doll's House."" — the news arrives first, one line, no comment after it, and then the page turns without a pause to "I am sure that meditation is the cure for the sickness of my mind, i.e. its lack of control."

The two sit on the same page and neither one looks at the other. A story taken, and directly beneath it a sentence that does not celebrate anything.

"I have a terribly sensitive mind which receives every impression, and that is the reason why I am so carried away and borne under." It is written as an explanation, as if the cause were settled and only the cure still open. I keep wondering whether "receives every impression" was meant as the trouble alone, or whether some part of you knew it was also the thing that took the story to The Nation.
