"Washed my hair. L.M. out all day. Here alone — a perfect day." The whole of it is set down in that register — one thing, then the next thing, no seam between the hair and the perfection.

Then the garden, and "There was a ship, white and solid on the water. Overcoat disappeared." The ship gets two adjectives and the overcoat gets none, and neither is explained. "The fire in my room and the double light."

And at the end, without warning, a voice that is not yours: " Good-bye." Then "It now believes we are going and it is safe." I keep circling the word *it*. Whatever *it* is, it was listening — did the house have to be told, or only the day?
