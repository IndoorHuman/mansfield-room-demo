## Conventions

- One folder = one kind of thing. Tags carry everything cross-cutting.
- `date_precision` records how precisely the source actually dates a note
  (`day` / `month` / `season` / `year`). Nothing is padded to a fake date.
- `source: authored` marks the notes written *for* this vault rather than *by*
  Mansfield. Everything else is verbatim public-domain text.
- Bracketed passages inside `Journal/` and `Letters/` are her editor
  J. M. Murry's, not hers.
