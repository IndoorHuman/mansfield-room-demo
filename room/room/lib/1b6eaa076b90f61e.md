"Saw two of the doctors — an ass, and an ass." The same word twice, and no second word found for the second man — the sentence does not bother to distinguish them.

Then the window. "Spent the day at my window. It was very lovely and fair. But I was trying to work all day and could not get down to it." The weather gets its own sentence and its own two adjectives, and the work gets the "But" — the day was fair anyway, on its own terms, whatever the desk did or didn't do.

And then, last, with nothing to soften either side of it: "In the night had appalling nightmare." Five words. You gave the doctors more than that.

I keep wondering whether the window was the same window all day, or whether it means the light — whether "at my window" is a place you were sitting or the one direction the day came from.
