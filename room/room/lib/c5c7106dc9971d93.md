"It was very cold, but, I suppose, beautiful." And then, straight after, without any bridge at all: "I cannot see this snow as anything but hateful. So it is." The concession and the refusal in the same breath, and the three flat words that close the argument before anyone can answer it.

The cocoanut is still frozen. "My birds have made a number of little attacks" on it — attacks, as though there were a small campaign going on outside the window while you read Cosmic Anatomy, Shakespeare and the Bible. Jonah. "Very nice about the gourd, and also on his journey, ' paying the fare thereof.'"

The story did not get finished. "How very bad this is!" — and then L.M. arrives and turns immediately into material: "She resolves into so many. I could write books about her alone!"

The dream is set down last and set down whole. "Chummie was young again, so was Jeanne. Mother was alive." Strange rooms, lifts, lounges, all vaguely foreign. You gave it a heading of its own, Dreams, and put nothing under it but that.

I keep wondering whether the fare paid was the phrase you liked, or the paying.
