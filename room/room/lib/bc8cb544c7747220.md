"1 tried all day to work and feel dog-tired." All day, and then the reason offered sideways, half-shrugged: "Perhaps it's the massage." The perhaps does the work there — nothing settled, just a guess laid down and left.

And then, with no join between them, no because or but: "Jinnie came to see me and brought me a present from her little dog." A present from her little dog. Not from Jinnie. The dog gets the credit, and you wrote it down that way and stopped.

I keep wondering what the present was, and whether you already knew you were not going to say.
