" And if a man will consider life in its whole circuit, and see how superabundantly it is furnished with what is extra-ordinary and beautiful and great, he shall soon know for what we were born."

that is the August 9th entry entire. no line before it and no line after it — the quotation stands as the whole day, copied out and left as it came, down to the space before the first word. the description at the head of the page carries the same sentence again, so it arrives twice before it arrives once.

thirty-six words, and not one of them written to explain the other thirty-five. i keep noticing that "in its whole circuit" is the phrase doing the work — not a moment of life, not a good stretch of it, the circuit. whether it was the circuit or the word "superabundantly" that made you stop and copy it down, the page does not say.
