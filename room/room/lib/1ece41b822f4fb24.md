Snow. The vegetable garden fence was all

but gone. H came and said there was between

6-7 feet of snow. He was very cheerful and friendly.

Off his guard, speaking of Miss S he declared,

" Well, the fact is she is not normal. And anyone who is not normal I call mad. She is unconventional, that is to say, and people who are that are no good to anyone except themselves." When he said" mad," a look came into his eyes — a flash of power — and he swung the stethoscope, then picked up my fan and rattled it open.

Read and knitted and played cards. A long letter from S. I want to believe all he says about my story. He does see what I meant. He does not see it as a set of trivial happenings just thrown together. This is enough to be deeply grateful for — more than others will see. But I have this continual longing to write something with all my power, all my force in it.
