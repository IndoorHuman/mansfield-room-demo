The twentieth of June 1918.

C'est de la misere.

Non, pas ca exactement. II y a quelque chose — une profonde malaise me suive comme un ombre.

Oh, why write bad French? Why write at all? 1 1,500 miles are so many — too many by 11,449 4 for me. [New Zealand is that distance from England.]
