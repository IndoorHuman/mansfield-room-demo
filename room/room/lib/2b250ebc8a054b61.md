# Short Fiction MOC

The 63 stories in this vault, by collection. All are verbatim public-domain
text from Project Gutenberg.

## In a German Pension (1911) — 13 stories
Her first book, written after a miscarriage in Bavaria. She refused to let it
be reprinted: *"It is positively juvenile."* Sharp, satirical, and much colder
than what came after.

## Bliss, and Other Stories (1920) — 14 stories
The book that made her. Contains **[[Bliss]]**, **[[Prelude]]**,
**[[Je ne Parle pas Français]]**, **[[The Man without a Temperament]]**.

## The Garden Party, and Other Stories (1922) — 15 stories
Her masterpiece, and the last book published in her lifetime. Contains
**[[At the Bay]]**, **[[The Garden-Party]]**,
**[[The Daughters of the Late Colonel]]**, **[[Miss Brill]]**,
**[[Life of Ma Parker]]**, **[[The Voyage]]**.

## The Doves' Nest, and Other Stories (1923) — 21 pieces
Published posthumously by Murry. Six finished stories — including
**[[The Fly]]**, **[[The Doll’s House]]**, **[[The Canary]]**,
**[[A Cup of Tea]]** — and fifteen unfinished fragments, which are worth
reading precisely because you can see the join.
