Long talk over house. Foster said I could walk. Sea sounded like an island sea. Happy. Lovely fire in my bedroom. Succes eclatant avec demon before dinner. Listened to Wingley's fiddle. The wooden bed.

i g20

January I. J. prepares to go. Drying figs on the stove, and white socks drying from the mantelpiece. A dish of oranges and rain-wet leaves — a pack of cards on the table. It rains but it is warm. The jonquil is in bud. We linger at the door. L.M. sings.
