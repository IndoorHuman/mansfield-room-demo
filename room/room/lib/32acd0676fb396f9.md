A horrible morning and afternoon. Je me sens incapable de tout, and at the same time I am just not writing very well. I must finish my story tomorrow. I ought to work at it all day, — yes, all day and into the night if necessary. A vile day. J'ai envie de prier au bon Dieu comme le vieux pere Tolstoi. Oh, Lord, make me a better creature to-morrow. Le cceur me monte aux levres d'un gout de sang. Je me deteste aujourd'hui. Dined at L.'s and talked the Island.1 It is quite real except that some part of me is blind to it. Six months ago I'd have jumped.

The chief thing I feel lately about myself is that I am getting old. I don't feel like a girl any more, or even like a young woman. I feel really quite past my prime. At times the fear of death is dreadful. I feel ever so much older than J. and that he recognises it, I am sure. He never used to, but now he often talks like a young man to an older woman. Well, perhaps, it's a good thing.
