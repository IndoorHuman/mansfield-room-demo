I can't tell you what a joy it was to see you yesterday, dear and tres tres belle amie. How I loved looking at you again. And hearing you. And seeing your home — everything. I so look forward to our meeting often this autumn. I do hope we may. Jack Murry sends his love. He's just had a new suit made and is standing in front of me.

/. M.: "Are the trousers full enough?"

K.M.: "Quite full enough!"/. M.:" You're sure? "

K. M.:u Certain!" J.M.:"They're not too full?"

K.M.:" Not in the least!" J.M.:"You're sure?"

K. M..-"Certain!" I must run and get a Bible and swear on it," Those trousers are perfect!! " Men are funny, aren't they? But very nice, too.
