Washed my hair. L.M. out all day. Here alone — a perfect day. I wandered in the garden.... There was a ship, white and solid on the water. Overcoat disappeared. The fire in my room and the double light. All was exquisitely beautiful. " Good-bye." It now believes we are going and it is safe.
