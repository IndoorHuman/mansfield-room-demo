# The Journal MOC

206 entries, 1914–1922, from the *Journal of Katherine Mansfield* (1927),
edited by J. M. Murry. **This folder is fenced** — see `CLAUDE.md`. This map
describes the diary; it does not quote it.

## The years
| Years | What's in them |
|-------|----------------|
| 1914–1915 | Paris and London. Poverty, restlessness, the beginning of the notebooks. Her brother arrives — and dies. |
| 1916–1917 | Cornwall and Garsington. The turn back toward New Zealand as her subject. |
| 1918–1919 | Illness becomes the fact of her life. Marriage to Murry. Her mother dies. |
| 1920–1921 | Menton, Montana, Sierre. The most productive years, written inside the illness. |
| 1922 | The last full year. Paris, Fontainebleau, and the decision to stop trying to be cured. |

## What Murry's brackets are
Every `[bracketed passage]` in these entries is **the editor's**, not hers —
Murry supplying places, names, and outcomes. Useful context; not her voice.
