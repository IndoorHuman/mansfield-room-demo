"Black. Another of them." Four words and then a count, as though this one had a place in a series you were already keeping without saying so anywhere else on the page.

What follows does not argue with it. "In the afternoon Foster came and agreed I must leave here. Somehow or other I wrote a column. Broke my watch glass." The column and the watch glass sit in the same run of sentences, the same weight, no line drawn between what was done and what was broken. "Somehow or other" is the only place the effort shows.

Then: "In the evening L.M. and I were more nearly friendly than we have been for years." Nearly, and years — you did not round it up.

"I couldn't rest or sleep. The roaring of the sea was insufferable." The sea comes last, and it gets the strongest word in the entry.
