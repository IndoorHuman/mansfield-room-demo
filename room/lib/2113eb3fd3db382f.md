Won a moral victory this morning, to my great relief. Went out to spend 2s. nd. and left it unspent. But I have never known a more hideous day. Terribly lonely. Nothing that isn't satirical is really true for me to write just now. If I try to find things lovely, I turn pretty-pretty. And at the same time I am so frightened of writing mockery for satire that my pen hovers and won't settle. Dined with C.'s and D. Afterward to Cafe Royal. The sheep were bleating and we set up a feeble counterpart. Saw a fight. The woman with her back to me — her arms crooked sharp at the elbows, her head thrust out, like a big bird.
