And then, in the same breath, the finishing and the not-knowing sit down together: "Actually finished the story, Brave Love,1 and I don't know what to make of it even now." The thing is done and still unaccounted for, and you did not resolve it before moving on.

Then it goes out of your hands and stays unresolved there too — "Read it to J. who was also puzzled." Two people puzzled is somehow steadier than one. I wonder what the puzzlement sounded like out loud, whether it went quiet or turned into laughing.
