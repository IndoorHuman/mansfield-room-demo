"I'd like to write a long, long story on this and call it " Last Words to Life." One ought to write it. And another on the subject of Hate."

The title arrives before the story does. Not a plan for one — two, and the second one only named, with nothing around it, as though naming were already most of the work.

"One ought to write it." You did not write *I ought*; you wrote *one*, which lets the sentence stand a little away from you, the way a thing said aloud in a room stands away from the person who said it.

What is the *this* the long, long story would be on? It points back at something you did not set down here.
