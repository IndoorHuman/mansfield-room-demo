The heavens opened for the sunset to-night.

When I had thought the day folded and sealed, came a burst of heavenly bright petals.... I sat behind the window, pricked with rain, and looked until that hard thing in my breast melted and broke into the smallest fountain, murmuring as aforetime, and I drank the sky and the whisper. Now who is to decide between "Let it be" and "Force it"? J. believes in the whip: he says his steed has plenty of strength, but it is idle and shies at such a journey in prospect. I feel, if mine does not gallop and dance at free will, I am not riding at all, but just swinging from its tail. For example, to-day.... To-night he's all sparks.
