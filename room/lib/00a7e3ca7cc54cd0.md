"I fell in love with a tree." It sits in the middle of the walk, between the pale violets and the japonica, with no explaining after it — the palms get "The beauty of palm trees" and the tree gets love, and the sentence moves on as though that were an ordinary thing to report from a garden.

The last line turns outward and complains on the flower's behalf: "Japonica is a lovely flower, but people never grow enough of it." I keep wondering which tree it was, and whether you would have said so if asked.
