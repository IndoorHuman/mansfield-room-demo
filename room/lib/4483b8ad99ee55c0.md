I meant, only the first chapter, not the " confession." No, I don't think that's a bit too " tragic." I can assure you I never stick pins into my cat; he's far more likely to stick pins into me.

And the reason why I used the "florid" image ' was that I was writing about a garden party. It seemed natural, then, that the day should close like a flower. People had been looking at flowers all the afternoon, you see.

Thank you for your delightful letter. I shall write en quelques jours. Just for the moment I'm having rather a fight with the rayons X.
