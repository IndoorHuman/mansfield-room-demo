# Things she disliked

> Every line below is **verbatim** from the 1929 *Letters* in this vault.
> Nothing here is drawn from the fenced diary. The groupings are the only
> authored part.

## Cold
> "I hate snow and icicles, and blizzards."

## Noise and interruption
> "I am so sensitive to noise, oh, so sensitive. It hurts me, really."

## Being managed
> "How I hate having to do this, especially as they have been so immoderately
> kind."

## Concealment, and mystery for its own sake
> "I hate people who hint at secrets in letters."

> "It always seems to me a kind of humbug — a justification of mystery, and I
> hate mystery."

## Her own early work
> "It is far too immature, and I don't even acknowledge it to-day."
> *(on* In a German Pension *, refusing a reprint)*

## The self-regarding age
> "People to-day are simply cursed by what I call the personal.... What is
> happening to ME. Look at ME. This is what has been done to ME."

## And, flatly
> "After Italy it seemed all right at first but then they began feeding us on
> haricot beans and I hate haricot beans."

## Sentimentality about suffering
> "I can't help seeing all the evil and pain in the world: it must be faced and
> recognised, and I can't bear your sentimentalist or silly optimist."
