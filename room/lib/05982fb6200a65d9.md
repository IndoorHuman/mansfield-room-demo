I hope this pen works. Yes, it does.

The last day in September — immensely cold, a kind of solid cold outside the windows. My lire has played traitor nearly all day and I have been, in the good, old-fashioned way, feeling my skin curl.

Don't read this. Do you hear that train whistle and now the leaves — the dry leaves — and now the fire

— fluttering and breaking.

Why doesn't she bring the lamps?
