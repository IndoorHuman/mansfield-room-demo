No letters or papers. V. came; and Mrs. V. and Miss S. in white. " The trouble I've had with you, Mrs. Murry, and the expense it's put me to — more fuss than if you'd died there." The women against the flowers were so lovely — even Miss S. I had a dreadful crying fit about " noise and cleanliness." It was horrible.
