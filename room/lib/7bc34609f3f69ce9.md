"Nuit blanche. Decided at 3 a.m. that D. was a homicidal maniac. Certain of this." Then, with no break at all, no paragraph, no breath: "Started my story, Late Spring.'"

The 3 a.m. certainty gets four words of aftermath and no argument. You do not walk it back later in the entry, and you do not explain it either. It stands, and the next sentence is the story beginning.

What the day gets instead is hours. "Worked on Tchehov all day and then at my story till 11 p.m." Two kinds of work, both of them yours, and the clock said out loud at the end of it.

And then the entry does something I keep turning over. After the strike, after "No letters," the last thing set down is "Anna's bow and velvet blouse." Not what Anna said, not what was talked about to her face in English — the bow, the velvet. Is that what stays when a night has gone like that, the thing someone else was wearing?
