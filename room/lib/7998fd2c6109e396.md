A letter from F. I had not expected it, and yet, when it came, it seemed quite inevitable — the writing, the way the letters were made, his confidence, and his warm sensational life. I wish he were my friend; he's very near to me. His personality comes right through his letters to J. and I want to laugh and run into the road.
