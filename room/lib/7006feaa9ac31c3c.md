# New Zealand MOC

She left Wellington in 1908 and never went back. Then she spent the rest of her
life writing it — deliberately, as work, after her brother's death in 1915 gave
her a reason to go looking for their shared childhood.

> "A young country is a real heritage, though it takes one time to recognise
> it. But New Zealand is in my very bones. What wouldn't I give to have a look
> at it!"

> "And another thing is the longer I live the more I turn to New Zealand."

> "I thank God I was born in New Zealand."

> "It's a sad pity that New Zealand is so far, dearest Papa."
> *(to her father)*

*(All verbatim from the 1929* Letters *.)*
