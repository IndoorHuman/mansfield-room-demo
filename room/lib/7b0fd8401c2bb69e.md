"The snow lay like silver light on the tops of the mountains." That is the second line of the page, and the last thing on the page, hours later, is the same snow again: "huge, glittering, with snow like silver light on their tops." Between the two, everything else swaps places. "the wide motionless rivers looked as though they were solid, and the pale furrowed earth, with white fruit-trees like coral branches, looked as if it were water." Rivers gone hard, fields gone liquid, and no comment made about it.

Then: "Later. The station clock." Nothing more. Whatever the clock said, it stayed with the clock.

After that come the unposted letters, three of them in a row. The first is a summons written against yourself, with the terms all laid out — "a weekly paper that pays you £950 a year for an article, personal as possible, the more intimate the better" — and then the choice: "(a) your reasons for withholding it (as subtle as you like) or (b) when she may expect it." The joke is precise about the fee and precise about the deadline and leaves the actual answer blank.

The second one is nearly all somebody else's dancing and one goldfinch. "He's jet tame, and this morning after it had rained he came for his Huntley and Palmer crumb with a little twinkling rain-drop on his head. I never saw anyone look more silly and nice." Anyone, not anything. The Swiss birds get no such courtesy: "stodgy little German trots flown out of Appenrodt's catalogue."

The third opens by refusing to open. "I keep walking and walking round this letter, treading on my toes and with my tail in the air; I don't know where to settle. There's so much to say and the day is so fine." And then "Well, here goes, darling" — and what comes out is the green velvet chair, L.M. seeing to things, and the wait. "I suppose we had a long wait there; it did not seem long." I keep wondering whether it was the mountains that did that to the hour, since they are what the sentence turns to immediately afterward, or whether the wait simply wasn't the thing you were in.
