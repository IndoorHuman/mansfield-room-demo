# The people in her life

> **Note on sources.** Authored reference note. Relationships and dates are
> public biographical fact; quotations are verbatim from the 1929 *Letters*
> and 1927 *Journal* in this vault.

## Family
- **John Middleton Murry ("Jack", "J.")** — critic, editor of *The Athenaeum*
  and *Rhythm*; her partner from 1911, husband from 1918. He edited the
  *Journal* (1927) and *Letters* (1929) that this vault is built from — so
  every bracketed aside in the diary is his voice, not hers.
- **Leslie Heron Beauchamp ("Chummie")** — her younger brother, killed in
  France in October 1915. His death is the hinge of her writing life.
- **Sir Harold Beauchamp** — her father, banker; she wrote to him formally and
  often. Six letters in this vault.
- **Annie Burnell Beauchamp** — her mother, who died in 1918. Model for Linda
  Burnell. See [[annie-beauchamp-mother]].
- **Richard Murry ("Arthur")** — Jack's younger brother, a painter. Fourteen
  letters here, and the best ones: she talks to him about *how to make things*.

## The circle
- **Ida Baker ("L.M.", "the Mountain")** — her devoted, exasperating lifelong
  companion and nurse.
- **S. S. Koteliansky ("Kot")** — Russian translator; collaborator and confidant.
- **Lady Ottoline Morrell** — Garsington's hostess; eight letters here.
- **D. H. Lawrence and Frieda** — friends, neighbours, and combatants.
- **Sydney and Violet Schiff** — patrons and friends of her last years.
- **William Gerhardi, Anne Estelle Rice, Sylvia Lynd, Dorothy Brett** — the
  working friendships of the last three years.

## The dead she wrote toward
- **Anton Tchehov (Chekhov)** — not an acquaintance but the presence she
  measured herself against and defended: *"People on the whole understand
  Tchekhov very little."* See [[On Tchehov]].
