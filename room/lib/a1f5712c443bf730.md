"M. came, wearing her woolly lamb." That is how the page opens, with the coat before the person, and then straight into "A strange fate overtakes me with her. We seem to be always talking of physical subjects."

You set the whole visit down as a thing that keeps happening rather than a thing you chose: "They bore and disgust me, for I feel it is waste of time, and yet we always revert to them." And still you kept the details — she "lay back on the pillows, talking," she "had an absent air," she "was saying how fine women were." Whatever the subject was doing to you, you were watching closely enough to keep her posture.

Then the sentence that turns on itself: "it was on the tip of my tongue to be indiscreet. But I was not. Thank Heaven!" Three beats, and the last one arriving fast. I notice you did not write down what the indiscretion would have been. It stays a shape with nothing inside it, and the page moves on.

What comes after is plain and has no scene around it. "I have been in pain, in bad pain all day. I ache all over. I can hardly stand." You said it twice over, pain and then ache, as if once did not cover it. And the last line does not resolve anything: "It seems impossible that I am going away on Monday."

The visit and the pain are in the same entry with nothing joining them — no paragraph explaining that you were in pain while she talked. Did the two feel separate to you as you wrote, or only look separate on the page?
